# Auckland vegan dining research — expanded catalogue

Research pages were retrieved on **2026-10-09**. The catalogue contains **44 venue locations and 47 named dish records**, with coverage across Central Auckland, the North Shore, East and South Auckland, West Auckland, and Riverhead. The locations include cafes, restaurants, bars and gastropubs; five &Sushi locations are separately listed because each has its own address.

`vegan: true` means the cited source marks that specific item vegan, identifies the entire venue/menu as vegan, or gives a direct vegan customization for that item. `glutenFree: true` is used only where the cited source explicitly labels that same item gluten-free / made without gluten, or directly offers a GF customization for it. `glutenFree: false` means no GF claim was verified; it does not mean the item contains gluten. The catalogue includes venue menus and, where the official menu exposed insufficient dish detail, a small number of current third-party ordering listings; these can differ from dine-in menus.

Three dish records are marked `needs-checking`: Gojo’s GF injera pairing is only reported in a third-party customer review and is not confirmed by its current official menu; Westie Bistro’s explicitly VGN/GF item is from a September 2024 menu PDF; and Auckland Art Gallery Café’s vegan item comes from a May 2025 PDF. The latter two are retained as useful leads but the menu age means current availability should be checked. No external review is represented as a user visit report. All other dish records are marked `menu-supported` based on a retrieved menu or ordering listing.

High-chair availability is `available` for Postal Service Cafe based on a First Table listing; all other venues remain `unknown` because no explicit venue evidence was found in this research pass. Coordinates are present only for Café Botannix Pakuranga, from its official cafe locator; the rest are null. Address provenance is through official venue sites/menu pages when available, otherwise the cited menu listing. The three Auckland Zoo outlets share the official Zoo street address, with their individual in-Zoo sites identified in venue notes. No venues were contacted.

Some Auckland Zoo café menu PDFs are late-2025 updates; they explicitly identify vegan / made-without-gluten items, but seasonal availability can change. BOWL’D’s menu explicitly warns that traces of gluten may be present because food is prepared in a kitchen handling gluten. THIRTEEN50 and East also have explicit shared-kitchen/allergen caveats in their notes. For any allergy-sensitive dining, a menu label does not establish cross-contact safety.

`research/unverified-candidates.json` preserves leads where current dish-level vegan/GF status was not established, including general GF availability and listings that did not expose a named item with sufficient evidence.
